# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We aim to make participation in this project welcoming, respectful, and
constructive for everyone.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and considerate.
- Welcome different perspectives and experience levels.
- Give constructive feedback and focus on the work, not the person.
- Respect other participants and their time.
- Report concerns privately to the project maintainers.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discriminatory language, personal attacks, intimidation, and
unwelcome abusive conduct are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these
standards. They may remove contributions or restrict participation in
response to unacceptable behavior.

## Scope

This code of conduct applies to project spaces and interactions related to
the project.

## Questions

Contact the repository maintainers with questions or concerns.
